# Tools

Every tool is listed with its parameters and the fields of its result. A parameter marked `*` is required.
Access is what the tool annotations declare: `read-only` changes nothing, `write` changes a project or writes
files, `destructive` can delete or overwrite (a comp, a movie, a layer, unsaved work, or whatever a script
does). A tool that runs a packaged script saves its `project` only when the script changed it, and returns the
script's report as `report` with its fields repeated at the top of the result. A `project` with no file is created only from an
untitled, empty open project; `ae_project` `new` creates one otherwise. Paths
are absolute. Layers are a 1-based index or a name. Colors are RGB from 0 to 1.

A tool that fails raises an error with the reason; a script's `errors` field fails the call. The error shape and
codes are in [Results and errors](results-and-errors.md).

## Session

| Tool | Access | Parameters | Result | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ae_status` | read-only |  | `app`, `version`, `aerender`, `headless_pids`, `ui_pids`, `error` | The installed application, its version and running pids. A missing application is reported in `error`. |
| `ae_launch` | write |  | `app`, `version`, `aerender`, `headless_pids`, `ui_pids`, `error` | Starts headless After Effects, or uses the one running with its window, and waits until it answers scripts. |
| `ae_script` | destructive | `script`*, `project`*, `args`, `timeout` | `project`, `report` | Runs a `.jsx` with the global `CAVI_ARGS`; it calls `caviReport(obj)`. `timeout` is 10 to 3600 seconds, default 900. |

| `ae_project_settings` | write | `project`*, `bits_per_channel`, `linear_blending`, `working_space`, `display_start_frame`, `time_display`, `frames_count`, `expression_engine`, `gpu` | `project`, `report`, `settings` | Sets project-wide bit depth, color space, time display, expression engine and GPU mode. |

| `ae_batch` | destructive | `project`*, `steps`*, `timeout` | `project`, `report`, `steps` | Runs up to 200 steps, each `{tool, args}` without `project`, in one After Effects call. Takes the typed write tools that run one script: comps, layers, text, shapes, masks, cameras, materials, markers, keyframes, expressions, effects, controls, audio, time, transitions, Essential Graphics, render queue entries, project settings, import, footage replacement and organizing. |

| `ae_font_replace` | write | `project`*, `font`*, `to`*, `fallback` | `project`, `report`, `font`, `to`, `replaced`, `layers` | Replaces every use of a font in the project's text, mixed styles included, with an installed font, and lists the layers that used it. `fallback` (default true) lets After Effects pick a font for characters `to` has no glyphs for. | `ae_effects` | read-only | `project`*, `filter`, `category`, `limit` | `project`, `report`, `effects`, `total`, `categories` | Lists installed effects with their match names. |
| `ae_presets` | read-only | `project`*, `filter`, `limit` | `project`, `report`, `render_settings`, `output_modules`, `animation_presets` | Lists render settings and output module templates and installed `.ffx` presets. |

## Comps and layers

| Tool | Access | Parameters | Result | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ae_comp_new` | destructive | `project`*, `name`*, `width`, `height`, `duration`, `fps`, `background`, `renderer`, `replace`, `footage` | `project`, `report`, `comp`, `renderer`, `replaced` | Creates a comp; `replace` removes an existing comp of that name. Without `footage`, `width`, `height` and `duration` are required; with it, the comp takes the file's size, pixel aspect, duration and frame rate where not given and holds it as layer 1, and a still needs `duration`. |
| `ae_comp_set` | write | `project`*, `comp`*, `name`, `width`, `height`, `duration`, `fps`, `work_area`, `background`, `renderer`, `motion_blur`, `frame_blending`, `duplicate_as` | `project`, `report`, `comp`, `source`, `duplicated`, `width`, `height`, `duration`, `fps` | Changes a comp, or a copy of it with `duplicate_as`. |
| `ae_layer_add` | write | `project`*, `layer`* | `project`, `report`, `layer` | Adds a solid, text, footage, 3D model, parametric mesh (`kind` `mesh` with `mesh_type` cube, sphere, plane, cylinder, cone or torus, sized by its `mesh` options, such as `width` or `radius`, with `bevel` on a cube, cylinder or cone; After Effects 26.3 or later), nested comp, rectangle, null, camera, light or adjustment layer. |
| `ae_layer_set` | write | `project`*, `comp`*, `layer`*, `name`, `enabled`, `solo`, `shy`, `locked`, `label`, `in_point`, `out_point`, `start_time`, `stretch`, `blend_mode`, `parent`, `no_parent`, `three_d`, `motion_blur`, `track_matte`, `collapse_transformation`, `guide`, `auto_orient`, `quality`, `sampling`, `adjustment`, `properties`, `mesh`, `bevel` | `project`, `report`, `comp`, `layer`, `mesh`, `properties_set`, `values` | Changes switches, timing, blend mode, parent, matte and static property values, such as `Geometry Options/Extrusion Depth` and `Geometry Options/Bevel Depth` of a 3D text or shape layer in an Advanced 3D or Cinema 4D comp. `mesh` and `bevel` set a parametric mesh's Mesh Options and Bevel Options for its own type; an option its type lacks is refused with the type's options, and `mesh` in the result holds the values read back (After Effects 26.3+). Collapse, guide, quality, sampling and adjustment are refused on cameras and lights; `report.switches` holds their values after the call. A menu property, such as `Effects/Fractal Noise/Fractal Type`, takes its item text or its 1-based index; an item it lacks is refused with its items (After Effects 26.0+). |
| `ae_layer_edit` | destructive | `project`*, `comp`*, `layers`*, `action`*, `time`, `move_to`, `name`, `to_comp` | `project`, `report`, `comp`, `action`, `layers`, `layer_count`, `to_comp` | Duplicates, deletes, reorders, splits or precomposes layers, or copies them with their keyframes, effects and masks to the top of `to_comp`, in order; `layers` and `layer_count` then describe `to_comp`. |
| `ae_layer_parts` | destructive | `project`*, `comp`*, `layer`*, `edits`* | `project`, `report`, `comp`, `layer`, `groups` | Runs `edits` in order, each `{path, action, to}`: `remove`, `enable`, `disable` or `move` (to a 1-based position) an effect, mask, shape group or text animator by path, such as `Effects/Glow`, `Masks/Mask 1`, `Contents/Group 1` or `Text/Animators/Animator 1`. Masks have no on/off switch. `groups` lists each changed group's members with `index`, `name`, `match_name` and `enabled`. |
| `ae_text_set` | write | `project`*, `comp`*, `layer`*, `text`, `font`, `size`, `fill`, `stroke`, `stroke_width`, `tracking`, `leading`, `justification`, `box`, `box_fit`, `box_align`, `box_inset`, `ranges` | `project`, `report`, `comp`, `layer`, `text`, `animators`, `overflow` | Replaces the text or style of a text layer. On paragraph text, `box_fit` grows the box down to fit the text (`cursor`, `bounds` or `baseline`; `none` stops), only with `box_align` `top`; `box_align` sets the vertical alignment and `box_inset` the inner margin; `overflow` is true when text does not fit the box and null for point text. With Source Text keyframes the style applies to every key and each key keeps its own text unless `text` is given; `report.keys` counts them. `ranges` styles characters `[start, end)` after the whole text, each with `font`, `size`, `fill`, `stroke`, `stroke_width`, `tracking`, `bold`, `italic` and `baseline_shift`; a range past the end of the text or of a key is refused, and `report.ranges` reads each range back, with null for a value its characters do not share. Whole-text report fields read the first character. |
| `ae_shape_add` | write | `project`*, `comp`*, `shape`*, `name`, `layer`, `group_name`, `fill`, `stroke`, `gradient_fill`, `gradient_stroke`, `operators`, `transform` | `project`, `report`, `comp`, `layer`, `group`, `contents` | Adds a shape layer, or a group on one, with operators and paint. |
| `ae_mask` | write | `project`*, `comp`*, `layer`*, `shape`, `mask`, `name`, `mode`, `feather`, `expansion`, `opacity`, `invert`, `path_keys` | `project`, `report`, `comp`, `layer`, `mask`, `mask_count`, `path_keys` | Adds or changes a mask, including a keyframed path. `path_keys` repeating a time are refused. |
| `ae_camera` | write | `project`*, `comp`*, `camera`, `rig`, `light` | `project`, `report`, `comp` | Adds or changes a camera, an orbit rig and a light. An environment light's `source` is a 2D footage, still, solid or comp layer of the comp whose image lights the scene. |
| `ae_material` | write | `project`*, `comp`*, `layer`*, `material`* | `project`, `report`, `comp`, `layer`, `renderer`, `material` | Sets a 3D layer's material options. |
| `ae_markers` | write | `project`*, `comp`*, `layer`, `markers`, `grid`, `onsets`, `cuts`, `clear` | `project`, `report`, `comp`, `count`, `markers` | Sets markers, a beat grid and markers at an audio file's onsets or a video file's cuts; at most 5000 markers per call. `onsets` is `{file, offset, rise, floor, gap, comment}`: FFmpeg measures the level every 11.6 ms, and an onset is a level at least `floor` dB (-50) and `rise` dB (6) above the quietest of the previous 70 ms, at least `gap` seconds (0.1) after the last. `cuts` is `{file, offset, threshold, comment}`: a cut is a frame whose FFmpeg scene score, the change from the frame before from 0 to 1, reaches `threshold` (0.4). `offset` places the file's start in comp seconds. |

## Transitions

| Tool | Access | Parameters | Result | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ae_transitions` | read-only | `project`* | `kinds` | Lists the transition kinds this install has. |
| `ae_transition` | write | `project`*, `comp`*, `kind`*, `layer`, `edge`, `from_layer`, `to_layer`, `seconds`, `frames`, `color`, `ease` | `project`, `report` | Adds a transition on one layer's in or out point, or between two layers. Between two layers it runs on the upper one (`edge` in the report): the outgoing layer goes out, or the incoming one comes in, over the other; the outgoing layer must last to the end of the transition. A dip trims the outgoing layer's out point and the incoming layer's in point to its midpoint (`trimmed`). |
| `ae_sequence_layers` | write | `project`*, `comp`*, `items`*, `start`, `overlap`, `transition` | `project`, `report`, `layers`, `joins` | Places layers or footage files end to end, with a transition at each join; a transition needs an `overlap` of its length (half of it for a dip). A `duration` longer than the source left from the layer's in point is refused. |
